Quick Start Guide

How to Use InkFlow — Best Way to Create Handwritten Pages

Follow these simple steps to turn your own handwriting into realistic A4 notebook pages.

Back to Notebook ↑
1

Create Custom Font

Create your own custom handwriting font using http://calligraphr.com/. Download their template, write your letters, upload the scan, and download your generated .ttf or .otf font file.

2

Open InkFlow Website

After making your handwriting font, open the InkFlow website: https://deepanshubhartiya.github.io/texttohandwriting/.

3

Upload Your Font

In the control panel, click upload font (or drag & drop your file) under Custom Font and select the handwriting font you created. You can also choose your Pen Color and Paper Type.

4

Adjust Size & Word Spacing

Adjust Font Size, Vertical Alignment, Letter Spacing, and Word Spacing using the sliders or by typing directly into the PX input boxes so your handwriting sits naturally on the lines.

5

Type in Notebook Page

Click directly on the notebook page and type or paste your text. To switch between pages with a smooth 3D page-turn animation, hover your mouse cursor inside the notebook page and scroll down for the next page or scroll up for the previous page.

6

Export Your Notebook Page

Hover or tap Export Page at the bottom of the control panel to Download PDF, Download All Pages as PNG images, or enter a page number and click Select Page.
